# Product Goals & Success Metrics

## Goals

### Goal 1: Centralize Employee Requests
Replace fragmented request channels (email, WhatsApp, paper) with a single system where every request has a clear owner, status, and audit trail.

**Metric:** 100% of employee requests submitted through the platform within 3 months of rollout.

### Goal 2: Streamline Time Tracking
Provide a reliable workflow for recording, submitting, and reviewing working hours — replacing manual Excel sheets.

**Metric:** Timesheet submission rate of 95%+ on time, tracked through the system dashboard.

### Goal 3: Enforce Role-Based Access
Ensure employees, managers, and HR each see only what they're authorized to see — enforced at the backend level, not just the UI.

**Metric:** Zero unauthorized access incidents. All permissions verified through automated tests.

### Goal 4: Deliver a Responsive, Consistent Experience
The product must work seamlessly across desktop, tablet, and mobile with a unified design system.

**Metric:** Core employee flows (request submission, time logging) complete in under 2 minutes on mobile.

### Goal 5: Structured SpecKit Workflow
Demonstrate a disciplined, specification-led development process with traceability from requirement to specification to task to implementation to test.

**Metric:** Every feature has a linked spec, plan, task, and test — verifiable through commit history and documentation.
