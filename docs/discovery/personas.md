# User Personas

## Persona 1: The Employee

**Name:** Sarah Hassan
**Role:** Software Developer
**Age:** 28

**Goal:** Submit requests and log working time quickly without navigating bureaucracy.

**Pain Points:**
- Doesn't know the status of her leave request — has to ask her manager in person
- Logs hours in an Excel sheet that gets lost or overwritten
- No central place to see her request history or timesheet approvals
- Feels like requesting time off is a hassle with unclear process

**What Success Looks Like:**
- Opens the app, sees all her requests and their statuses at a glance
- Submits a leave request in under 2 minutes
- Logs daily time entries and submits a weekly timesheet with one click
- Gets notified when her request is approved or needs changes

---

## Persona 2: The Manager

**Name:** Ahmed Khalil
**Role:** Engineering Team Lead
**Age:** 35

**Goal:** Stay on top of team requests and timesheets without micromanaging.

**Pain Points:**
- Requests come through email, Slack, and verbal conversations — impossible to track
- Has to remind team members to submit timesheets every week
- No quick way to see who has pending leave during sprint planning
- Can't report on team hours without manually consolidating spreadsheets

**What Success Looks Like:**
- Opens the app and sees a dashboard with all pending team requests and timesheets
- Approves or rejects requests with one click and a comment
- Reviews and returns timesheets that need correction
- Filters by request type, status, or team member

---

## Persona 3: HR

**Name:** Mona Abdelrahman
**Role:** HR Director
**Age:** 42

**Goal:** Maintain organization-wide oversight, ensure compliance, and produce reports for leadership.

**Pain Points:**
- No visibility into how many leave requests are pending across the organization
- Can't track timesheet compliance — who submitted, who didn't
- Employee records are in one system, requests in another, timesheets in a third
- Can't configure request types or policies without IT support
- Producing monthly reports takes days of manual work

**What Success Looks Like:**
- Sees an organization-wide dashboard with key metrics (pending requests, timesheet compliance, leave trends)
- Manages employee records, reporting lines, and roles in one place
- Configures request types (leave, equipment, WFH, HR service, general) without developer help
- Generates reports on working hours and request activity by team, department, or period
- Has full audit trail of every action taken in the system
