# Problem Statement

## The Problem

Organizations today manage employee requests and time tracking through fragmented, disconnected channels — email threads, WhatsApp messages, paper forms, and scattered Excel sheets. This creates three critical problems:

1. **No visibility:** Employees don't know the status of their requests. Managers can't see what's pending. HR has no organization-wide view of workforce activity.

2. **No accountability:** When requests are handled informally, there's no audit trail. Who approved what? When? Was it within policy? Nobody can answer these questions reliably.

3. **No efficiency:** Managers spend hours chasing employees for timesheets, reviewing them manually, and following up on requests. HR can't produce reports without manually consolidating data from multiple sources.

## The Impact

- Employees waste time following up on request statuses
- Managers lack real-time visibility into team workload and pending approvals
- HR cannot produce accurate workforce reports or track compliance
- Decisions are made without data
- Compliance and audit requirements are difficult to meet

## The Solution

A single, secure workspace that centralizes employee requests and time tracking with role-based access. Employees get a simple way to submit requests and log hours. Managers get a dashboard to review and approve. HR gets organization-wide oversight and reporting — all in one place, all with a clear audit trail.
